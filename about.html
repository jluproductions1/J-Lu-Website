About

A Vocation Found in Film

The Story

John Lucas, J-Lu Productions

In the first couple years of working in media, I took on various projects that started a love for storytelling. This love was mainly found in sports — filming athletes finding their purpose and glorifying God through their work. But along this journey, I was able to make other Christ-centered projects that impacted me like never before.

This new fire for Christ has led to this rebuild of professionalism and the desire to share the Word of the Gospel through film. God gave me a gift that I want to be able to fulfill as a vocation in this life, saving as many souls as I can along the way.

J-Lu Productions is being rebuilt around that belief: a filmography business that is faith-rooted and truth-seeking, focused on creating work that brings others closer to Christ, and encourages them to seek that very truth through authentic storytelling.

What Guides the Work

Beauty, truth, and service.

Beauty

High-quality, thoughtful craft matters — especially when the goal is to reflect something greater than the frame itself.

Truth

Every project is built to tell an honest story first, so the people and faith behind it are represented with integrity.

Service

The work exists to serve others — encouraging people to seek truth and encounter Christ through what's on screen.

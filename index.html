The Next Chapter

Three years behind the camera.
One calling ahead.

For the past three years, I've worked as a freelance videographer, mostly on the sidelines of sports. But a handful of faith-centered projects along the way changed everything — they reminded me that storytelling can be a fire, one I'm called to carry so that stories of faith can spread and move hearts.

I'm a Catholic student at Ave Maria University, and I believe God has placed this work on my heart as a vocation. J-Lu Productions is being rebuilt around that calling: a videography business rooted in faith, focused on work that brings others closer to Christ.
